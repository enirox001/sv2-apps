TxCollection JDP benchmark

20 samples, release build, local regtest node.
sv2-apps #599 at 31d3c510; Bitcoin Core #35671 at d0c20776.

Old mempool mirror (sendrawtransaction -> JDP success):
Core 30: mean 995.779 ms, p50 995.597, p95 1004.300
Core 31: mean 995.455 ms, p50 996.340, p95 1000.442

TxCollection, transaction already in the node mempool:
sendrawtransaction: mean 1.796 ms, p50 1.680, p95 2.365
collectTxs + makeTemplate: mean 2.269 ms, p50 2.090, p95 3.087
end-to-end: mean 4.066 ms, p50 3.671, p95 5.247

TxCollection, transaction missing from the node mempool:
unknownTxPos: mean 1.144 ms, p50 1.068, p95 1.451
addMissingTxs + makeTemplate: mean 1.963 ms, p50 1.908, p95 2.428
combined: mean 3.108 ms, p50 3.007, p95 3.794

The mempool-hit path was about 245x faster than the old mirror path in this run.
The missing-transaction result does not include miner-to-JDS network latency.
